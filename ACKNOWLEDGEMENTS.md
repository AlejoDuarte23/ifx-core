When you distribute a compiled native or WebAssembly build, provide this notice, the MPL terms, and the corresponding IFX source, including the modified imported files. The source distribution contains these files. The viewer has a separate license; it does not change the MPL terms for this core.

The source-schema registry is generated by IFX from the official buildingSMART
EXPRESS definitions. `crates/step/schema/sources.json` records the source URLs
and SHA-256 hashes. The original schema documents are cached locally for table
regeneration; they are not included in the distributed source archive. The
registry generator and regression fixtures are original IFX additions.
